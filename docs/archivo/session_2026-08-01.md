## Findings that must not be rediscovered

**The two 6h gaps are permanent upstream Coinbase gaps.** 2025-10-25 15:00
and 2026-05-08 01:00 UTC. Verified across 55/55 pairs covering that time,
zero exceptions, and re-requesting the window today still returns nothing.
The bar before each shows collapsed volume. Not a fetcher bug. Cannot be
repaired. Never interpolate them; a strategy is blind and flat across them.

**Presence is not coverage, and span is not coverage either.** A 7-day PEPE
file counted as a complete year under an existence check. Then a flat
300-day minimum flagged TON, HYPE, MON and five others as missing when they
were complete — they were just young. The correct target is the pair's own
start from the daily panel, floored at the window.

**A sliding-window refresh that overwrites silently discards history at the
head.** Refetching 365 days a week later adds 7 days at the tail and drops 7
at the front. `save_csv` now merges. Side effect worth exploiting: Coinbase
only serves ~60 days at 300s granularity, so a daily refresh accumulates
5-minute history that cannot be bought. Every day `daily.py` does not run is
a day permanently lost.
