# Session notes — 2026-07-30

## Headline: the entrance exam could not discriminate

`--selftest` showed plain buy-and-hold FAILS its own exam on 3 of 5
criteria. Ledger stood at 10 candidates / 10 FAIL. Automated tally:

    5/5 fail  2_safety  (no window DD <= -20%)   <-- blocks everything
    5/5 fail  4_return  (stitched >= buy-hold)   <-- blocks everything
    1/5 fail  3_riskedge                          <-- actually discriminates

Root cause of criterion 4: the benchmark is a raw price ratio paying
ZERO fees while candidates pay 0.6%+0.05% per side. Measured: 0.65%
drag per window, ~53% of terminal wealth over 116 windows. Nothing that
pays fees can pass. See docs/exam_amendment_001.md (PROPOSED, unsigned).

## "More trading = more learning" — settled empirically

Screen of 12 strategies x 20 pairs, edge measured vs buy-and-hold net
of fees:

    corr(trades per window, edge) = -0.783

Monotonic and strong in the WRONG direction. voltgt_150 at 58
trades/window scores -33.7% edge; hold at 1 trade/window scores -0.81%.
Cost budget: at 1.30% per round trip, only ~8 round trips/year are
affordable before friction alone eats 10% of capital.

## Validation findings

- trend_200 permutation test: p = 0.0995. Its +286% is NOT
  distinguishable from shuffled-data chance.
- trend_200 bootstrap: mean per-window edge -12.94%,
  90% CI [-18.93%, -7.42%]. Entirely below zero; 0% of resamples
  positive. Robustly worse than buy-hold, not noise.
- MA plateau sweep: stitched swings -87% to +10417% across MA choice
  (knife-edge = noise), while edge stays negative at EVERY parameter
  (stable = real). Trust the edge metric, not the headline.
- Multiple testing: after 10 trials, luck alone yields Sharpe ~0.79.
  Any candidate must clear that before it counts as evidence.

## Findings
Crypto screen, 20 strategies x 20 coins, 0.60% fees:
  19 of 20 lose to buy-and-hold. Sole positive, ema_9_21 (+0.64%/win),
  was killed by validation within minutes:
      bootstrap on 8 major pairs: mean edge -12.91%,
      90% CI [-18.84%, -7.40%], 0% of resamples positive
      permutation p = 0.070 (not significant, before correction)
  Source of the illusion: the junk-altcoin tail. A fast filter that
  exits collapsing alts "beats" holding them. Survivorship bias in a
  costume. The research->validate->exam firewall caught it on its
  first real temptation, which is exactly what it was built for.

Stock screen, same 20 strategies, ~12yr, near-zero fees:
  buy-and-hold beat ALL 20. Win rates collapsed to 13-32% vs 50-68%
  in crypto. Caveat: 2014-2026 was a strong bull regime.
  Conclusion: in crypto fees kill timing; in stocks timing kills
  itself (returns concentrate in a few large days).

Regime filters tested (owner's "read the market first" idea):
  rsi_regime did WORSE than plain rsi_14 in both markets;
  calm_30_100 near the bottom of both. Opportunity cost > protection.
